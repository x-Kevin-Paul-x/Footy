# Stadium sound attribution

"Stadium Sound.wav" by cuauh89, August 17, 2012.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/cuauh89/sounds/163274/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Local file: stadium-cuauh89.mp3 (public high-quality MP3 preview).

Adaptations: filtered, resampled, looped with crossfades, mixed quietly,
ducked under commentary and modulated around recorded attacking events.
This is general stadium ambience, not an original recording of Turf Moor
or the simulated match. Retain this attribution with redistributed videos.
